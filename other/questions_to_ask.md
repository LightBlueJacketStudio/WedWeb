# checklist
✨ Actionable Checklist

When you build this page, you can use this quick checklist to make sure you haven't missed a thing:

Accommodation: List recommended hotels with booking links, group rates, and deadlines.

Travel: Identify nearest airports, best routes, and ground transportation options (shuttle, rental cars).

Directions: Provide a map or link from the airport to the hotel and venue.

Essentials: Mention weather, what to pack, local customs, and currency.

Local Fun: List your favorite nearby restaurants, beaches, and attractions.

Event Schedule: Outline the full weekend's itinerary (if more than just the wedding day).

Helpful Links: Add the hotel's website, a map link, and perhaps the local tourism board site.
